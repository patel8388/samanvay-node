# Samanvay Layer-1 and Wallet

## Outcome

Advance the existing no-value local prototype toward a sustainable, independently reviewed Layer-1 and wallet. Preserve the 10 billion SAMV cap, PoW mining plus staking rewards, mobile staking/mining support, public contribution access, and founder lifetime leadership without veto power.

## Delivery rule

Work in small, reviewed milestones. Every milestone must state what is functional, what is only a prototype, and which safety gates remain open. Current defaults remain local-only and tSAMV-only. Public networking, public testnet, mainnet, real assets, and fund movement are out of scope unless separately reviewed and explicitly authorized.
