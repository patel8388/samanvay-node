# Document open governance and founder leadership without veto

Translate the project's governance requirements into transparent public policy while keeping protocol validation independent of founder identity.

## Acceptance criteria

- Document lifelong founder leadership as the intended social/organizational role, explicitly without veto power over valid blocks, transactions, releases, or community contributions.
- Keep founder leadership separate from consensus permissions, treasury custody, grant decisions, and validator/miner rules.
- Define public contribution and review practices so anyone can participate through the public repository; state how contested protocol changes are handled.
- Do not implement founder-only consensus authority or claim that a social charter alone creates enforceable on-chain governance.
